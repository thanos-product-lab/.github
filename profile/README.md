# Thanos Product Lab

A place for the products and tools I build outside work.

I'm interested in software that makes complicated things easier to understand:
complex workflows, decision support, developer tools and applied AI.

The goal is to take ideas beyond the prototype stage, thoughtful UX,
real architecture, tests, evaluations, deployment and the less glamorous
details that make software feel finished.

---

## Projects

### 🏛️ Citizenship Workspace

**Shipped · September 2026**

A private workspace for preparing a UK citizenship application.

It explores a question I've been interested in for a while: how do you put
AI inside a product where being wrong matters, without letting its output
quietly become the truth?

The product keeps important decisions deterministic and traceable. AI can
read documents and suggest what it finds, but its output does not become
part of the case until the user confirms it.

## About the lab

I use this organisation as a place to explore product ideas and push beyond
the parts of engineering I work with every day.

That usually means taking ownership of the whole product: understanding the
problem, designing the experience, making architecture decisions, building
the frontend and backend, and figuring out where AI is actually useful.

Not every project needs AI. When it is used, I want it to have a clear job,
clear boundaries and a way to understand when it gets things wrong.
